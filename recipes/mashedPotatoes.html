Mashed Potatoes

Description

Soft, creamy, fluffy mashed potatoes.

Ingredients

  • 3-4 Potatoes
  • 1/2 Cup of heavy cream
  • 1 Rosemary sprig
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 3 Tbsp of butter
  • Seasoning of your liking

Steps

  1. Pre-heat the oven to 420 degrees Farenheit.
  2. Wash your potatoes and poke holes all around them using a fork.
  3. Once the oven is up to temperature, place the potatoes inside for 40 minutes.
  4. Melt a tablespoon of butter in a pot and mince the garlic.
  5. Place minced garlic in the pot, stirring frequently for 1-2 minutes or until golden and fragrant.
  6. Pour heavy cream into pot and raise heat to medium high.
  7. Once heavy cream starts to bubble, lower the heat to simmer. Place rosemary into the pot along with any other seasonings. Stir occasionally. Simmer for 20 minutes
  8. Pull out potatoes from oven and skin them if you would like. Place the potatoes in the pot of heavy cream after adding butter to the pot.
  9. Mash potatoes and mix well with heavy cream.
