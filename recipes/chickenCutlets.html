Chicken Cutlets

Description

Crispy golden fried chicken

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Breadcrumbs
  • Parmesan Cheese
  • Egg
  • Vegetable Oil
  • Seasonings

Steps

  1. Cut chicken into thin flanks. Tenderize them. Season them to your liking.
  2. Whisk an egg in a container to later dip the chicken in. Season the egg.
  3. Pour bread crumbs into a seperate container. Grate parmesan cheese onto the breadcrumbs. Season the breadcrumbs. Mix everything togther.
  4. Heat up oil in a deep pan with enough oil to barely cover the chicken.
  5. Dip Chicken into egg and then place into breadcrumbs. Cover both sides of the chicken. Pat each side to help the breadcrumbs stick.
  6. Fry chicken in the oil and flip once its golden brown. Fully cooked when the internal temperature reaches 165 degrees Farenheit.
  7. Place cooked chicken on a wire rack to cool.
