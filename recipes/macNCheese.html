Macaroni and Cheese

Description

Creamy and Cheesey Mac'N'Cheese

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • 2 or more kinds of cheese
  • Breadcrumbs
  • Butter
  • Flour
  • Heavy cream
  • Seasonings

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees Farenheit.
  2. Boil water for pasta. Salt water and then pour pasta in. Stir occasionally and cook until al dente.
  3. Drain pasta and save some pasta water.
  4. Melt butter in a pan. Slowly throw flour into the butter. Frequently stirring to keep the flour and butter from buring.
  5. After a couple minutes, slowly pour in heavy cream. Season to your liking. Keep stirring.
  6. Melt a quarter of your cheese into the sauce.
  7. Grate the rest of the cheese but keep aside. Mix pasta into the cheese sauce.
  8. In a deep pan, pour half the pasta sauce mixture into it. Then follow with almost a half of your cheese. Repeat again to layer the pasta and grated cheese. Cover the top of the pan with breadcrumbs and any leftover cheese.
  9. Place pan in over for 20-30 minutes. Broil for the last 5-10 minites to give the top extra crisp if need be.
  10. Remove pan out of the oven once done and let cool.
